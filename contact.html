Frequently Asked Questions

Before you ask

How do you ensure sustainability in your services? +
Sustainability is at the core of our operations. From design to production and transport, we use eco-friendly materials and energy-efficient processes to minimize the environmental impact.
What is the typical timeline for a project using your services? +
Thanks to offsite serial construction, most homes are produced in the factory and assembled on site in days — a POP UP in 1–2 days, a traditional home in just 1 day. Full projects are typically delivered within weeks instead of months.
Are your services customizable for unique project requirements? +
Yes. Every home is planned digitally in 3D and adapted to your land, climate and needs — from 28 m² POP UP units to multistory multifamily buildings and fully bespoke Signature Homes.
Do you provide assembly services on-site? +
Yes. Our experienced teams assemble the components on site with precision and speed — with no wet trades, minimal noise and almost no waste.
Can you handle the structural engineering (statics) of my project? +
Yes. We provide full structural engineering (statics), earthquake-resistant and fire-resistant structures, and certified materials from Germany, Austria & Switzerland.
How is transport managed for my project materials? +
Efficient and sustainable transport solutions ensure safe, on-schedule delivery of every component to your project location — engineered to comply with transport regulations worldwide.
