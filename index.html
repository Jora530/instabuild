Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How can modular buildings help reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions? ＋
Modular homes are built with high-performance insulation, airtight construction and KfW40 energy standards — reducing heating energy by up to 75% and dramatically cutting greenhouse gas emissions compared to conventional buildings.
How are InstaBuilt's walls different from traditional walls? ＋
Our walls are prefabricated CLT and panel systems with integrated insulation layers — precision-engineered in the factory for superior thermal performance, fire safety and assembly speed. Each wall is a complete, certified construction element, not a stack of on-site materials.
What is KfW40? ＋
KfW40 is a German energy efficiency standard: a KfW40 home consumes only 40% of the primary energy of a standard new building — meaning dramatically lower heating costs and a much smaller carbon footprint.
What is QNG? ＋
QNG (Qualitätssiegel Nachhaltiges Gebäude) is a German certification for sustainable buildings, awarded to structures that meet strict ecological, economic and social criteria — the highest recognition for sustainable construction.

To drive meaningful progress in our operations and supply chain, we focus on measurable actions across Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions.

Scope 1: Direct Emissions

We prioritize the reduction of emissions generated directly by our operations:

  • Optimizing modular production processes to minimize energy use on-site
  • Reducing reliance on fossil fuels through renewable energy integration in our facilities

Scope 2: Indirect Emissions from Energy Use

Our efforts to address indirect emissions focus on how we source and consume energy:

  • Utilizing renewable energy sources, including solar and wind power, to run our production plants and offices
  • Implementing energy-efficient lighting and climate control systems in all facilities
  • Conducting regular energy audits to identify areas for further reduction and optimization

Scope 3: Value Chain Emissions

We collaborate with suppliers, customers, and partners to reduce emissions across our entire value chain:

  • Sourcing sustainable and low-carbon materials from verified suppliers
  • Encouraging suppliers and partners to adopt eco-friendly practices aligned with global sustainability standards
  • Designing modular homes that reduce long-term energy consumption for end-users
